About Us

Welcome to the website for the Harvard-Radcliffe Asian-American Christian Fellowship (AACF)! We are a fellowship that seeks to know God and make God known as a voice of faith, particularly among the Asian and Asian-American community at Harvard. Pursuing personal relationships with Jesus and sharing God’s love on campus, AACF is devoted to the teaching of the Bible, prayer, worship, outreach, and community. We invite people of any faith background to explore and hear the Gospel and are eager to welcome you to our community!

We have weekly classwide Bible studies and Friday night large group gatherings where you can learn alongside your class and the entire fellowship. Some of our special events include semesterly retreats and fun socials, such as midnight hiking, apple picking, and game night. If you want more information and want to join us at these events, please read our upcoming events section!
